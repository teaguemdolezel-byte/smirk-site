The old move was: build a tool, charge a monthly fee, hope people use it. The new move is: do the job, use software to do it ten times faster, and charge for the job.

A business owner doesn’t want an email platform. They want revenue from the people who left. They don’t want a CRM. They want to know who to call today.

***

When you sell the outcome, the software becomes your leverage instead of their homework. And you learn exactly where the work breaks, because you’re the one doing it.

The question to ask before you build: does the customer want to use this, or do they want it handled?
