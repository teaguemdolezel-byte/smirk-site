Every system we build now has agents in it. They read, match, pull history, draft, flag. They’re fast and mostly right.

Mostly is the problem.

***

So: agents draft, people decide. Anything that touches money, a customer or a commitment waits for a human. The agent’s job is to make that look take ten seconds instead of ten minutes.
