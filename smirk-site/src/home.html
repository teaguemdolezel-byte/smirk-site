How we think

the same five, whatever we're building.

  1. See the whole thing

    Understand the business, not just the immediate problem.

  2. Find the friction

    Look for the handoffs, gaps and repetitive work.

  3. Build what matters

    The right solution isn't always more software.

  4. Connect the pieces

    A system is only as good as how its parts work together.

  5. Make it last

    Build for the people who have to use it every day.
